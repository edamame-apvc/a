👶 Toddler Tips

  • Major department stores (Takashimaya, Daimaru) have the best nursing & changing rooms (usually on kids clothing floors).
  • Train stations have priority elevators, but they can get crowded.
  • Convenience stores (Lawson, 7-11) are great for emergency snacks & wet wipes.
  • Always bring a small trash bag (public bins are very rare).
